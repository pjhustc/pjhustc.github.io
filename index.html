About Me

Welcome to Junhui Peng's personal site! Junhui is a postdoctoral researcher and a C. H. Li Memorial Scholar in Dr. Li Zhao's lab at Rockefeller University. My research focuses on how genetic novelty gives rise to novel proteins and protein-protein interactions—key components that shape the complex cellular machinery and drive phenotypic innovation.

To explore these questions, Junhui uses an multidisciplinary approach combining computational biophysics, molecular evolution, population genomics, multi-omics, and deep learning.
